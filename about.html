Introduction

Hewad Future Consultancy Company Over view

Established in 2020 in Kabul, Afghanistan, Hewad Future Consultancy (HFC) is a multidisciplinary professional consulting and advisory firm. We provide integrated advisory, consulting, technology, management, financial, risk, organizational development, and capacity-building services to public institutions, private companies, financial institutions, NGOs, development organizations, and entrepreneurs.

We help organizations understand and address complex challenges, develop practical solutions, manage risks, strengthen performance, embrace digital transformation, improve organizational capabilities, and achieve sustainable growth. Our approach combines strategic thinking, technical expertise, local knowledge, international best practices, evidence-based analysis, and practical implementation to deliver measurable, sustainable, and lasting results.

At HFC, we believe effective consulting goes beyond providing recommendations. We work closely with our clients from strategy development through implementation, monitoring, evaluation, and continuous improvement—helping transform ideas and strategies into practical results.

Mohammad Nasim Rasoli
HFC Company CEO
